# Literature and citation provenance

The bibliography is a retained research library; inclusion alone is not verification or endorsement of an entry's annotations. Only cited works appear in the rendered references. The working manuscript limits its substantive literature claims to the constructions it actually discusses.

Primary sources consulted for corrections:

- [Comrie, alignment of full noun phrases](https://wals.info/chapter/98): distinguishes S/A/P case marking and its domain.
- [Coecke, Sadrzadeh, and Clark, 2010](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.4394): categorical compositional distributional semantics.
- [Bradley, Terilla, and Vlassopoulos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07890): enriched language construction; corrected the previously incorrect third author.
- [Caramello, technical bridge explanation](https://www.oliviacaramello.com/Unification/TechnicalExplanation.html): equivalence of classifying toposes and invariant transport; profile counts are not a Morita test.
- [Leinster and Shulman, magnitude homology](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/files/190304831/1711.00802.pdf): graded invariant, distinct from the local cup/cap score.
- [Dabney et al., quantile regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10044): pairwise current-target loss.
- [Dabney et al., implicit quantile networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/dabney18a.html): corrected 2018 attribution and the distinction between a learned network and a finite pairwise-update helper.
- [Friston et al., active inference process theory](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27870614/): conceptual motivation; the local finite filter is a restricted example.
- [Akgul et al., distributional active inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20985): external RL/inference research; the local score utilities are not a reproduction or linguistic validation.
- [DisCoPy pregroup API](https://docs.discopy.org/en/main/_api/discopy.grammar.pregroup.Diagram.html): actual typed constructors and reductions.
