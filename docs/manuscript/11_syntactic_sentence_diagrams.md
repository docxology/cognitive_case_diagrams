# Appendix A: Schematic Construction Panel {#sec:syntactic-diagrams}

The panel collects ${syntactic_panel_sketch_count_word} hand-authored construction sketches. Its arcs are dependency-style annotations, not constituency trees produced by a parser. Some panels include a checked simple type template; the relative-clause and causative sketches display their interpretive structure without claiming a completed formal derivation.

![${syntactic_panel_sketch_count} pedagogical sketches spanning intransitive, transitive, ditransitive, passive, ergative, benefactive, relative-clause, and causative patterns. Words, roles, and arcs are supplied by the generator. The ergative example uses schematic labels rather than an unverified language-specific sentence. The English examples use assigned NOM/ACC/DAT/INS labels to illustrate argument functions; those labels do not assert corresponding English case inflections. A benefit recipient or passive agent should not be equated with a particular morphological case across languages. Readers seeking executable reductions should use the DisCoPy constructors in [@sec:categorial-grammar], whose types and codomains can be checked directly.
